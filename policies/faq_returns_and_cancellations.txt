## FAQ: Returns, Refunds, and Cancellations

This document contains **frequently asked questions (FAQ)** related to returns, refunds, and order cancellations.
It is designed to be used by a RAG system for **semantic search**.

---

### 1. Returns – General Questions

**Q1. How long do I have to return an item?**  
You can usually return an eligible item within **30 calendar days** from the **delivery date** shown in your order details.
After 30 days, the item is generally **not eligible** for return unless required by local consumer protection laws.

**Q2. In what condition should items be for a successful return?**  
Items should be in **original condition**, unused, and with all **original packaging, manuals, tags, and accessories**.
If items show signs of heavy use, missing parts, or damage, the return may be **rejected** or refunded only **partially**.

**Q3. Which items are non-returnable?**  
Some categories are normally **non-returnable**, including:
- Personalized or made-to-order items, unless they arrive damaged or defective.
- Digital goods and subscriptions that have already been accessed, used, or downloaded.
- Items clearly marked as **“Final Sale”** or **“Non-returnable”** at the time of purchase.

**Q4. Can I return a defective or damaged item?**  
Yes. If your item arrives **damaged, defective, or not as described**, you should initiate a **return request as soon as possible**.
In such cases, return shipping is often **free**, and you may be eligible for a **full refund** or **replacement**.

**Q5. Do I need the original packaging to return a product?**  
Original packaging is **strongly recommended**. It helps protect the item during return transit and speeds up inspection.
If you do not have the original packaging, your return may still be accepted, but the refund amount could be **reduced**
if the missing packaging or accessories significantly affect resale value.

---

### 2. Returns – Process and Approval

**Q6. How do I initiate a return?**  
To initiate a return:
1. Log in with your **registered email and password**.
2. Go to the **My Orders** section.
3. Select the relevant order and choose the **item(s) to return**.
4. Provide a **reason** for the return and any optional comments or photos.
5. Submit the request.

The system will create a **return request** and open a **support ticket**. You will receive an **email confirmation**
that your request is in **PENDING** status and will be reviewed by a human agent.

**Q7. Are returns automatically approved?**  
No. Returns are **not automatically approved**. A human **admin** reviews each return request to ensure it meets policy
criteria (e.g., within the 30-day window, correct item category, acceptable condition).
The admin may then approve, reject, or edit the request.

**Q8. How long does it take for my return to be approved or rejected?**  
Review times may vary, but most return requests are reviewed within **2–5 business days**.
You will be notified by email once the status changes to **APPROVED** or **REJECTED**.

**Q9. Can I change a return request after submitting it?**  
If your request is still in **PENDING** status, you may contact support or use the chatbot to **update or cancel** the
return request. Once the admin has taken action or the item has been shipped back, changes may no longer be possible.

---

### 3. Refunds

**Q10. When will I get my refund after returning an item?**  
Once the returned items are **received and inspected**, refunds are usually processed within **5–10 business days**.
The actual time for the refunded amount to appear in your account depends on your **bank or payment provider**.

**Q11. How will I receive my refund?**  
Whenever possible, refunds are made to the **original payment method** used for the purchase (e.g., credit card, UPI, wallet).
In special cases, refunds may be issued as **store credit** or **gift cards**.

**Q12. Will my original shipping fee be refunded?**  
This depends on the reason for return:
- If the item was **defective, damaged, or incorrectly shipped**, the **original shipping fee is often refunded**.
- If you are returning an item due to **change of mind** or personal preference, shipping fees are usually **not refunded**.

**Q13. Can I get a partial refund?**  
Yes. Partial refunds may occur when:
- Items show signs of heavy use or damage.
- Parts, accessories, or original packaging are missing.
- Only part of a multi-item order is returned.

---

### 4. Cancellations – Before Shipping

**Q14. Can I cancel my order before it is shipped?**  
In many cases, yes. If your order has **not yet been shipped**, you can usually cancel it directly from your account
or by asking the chatbot to **raise a cancellation request**.
Once the cancellation is processed, you should receive a **full refund**.

**Q15. How long do I have to cancel an order before shipping?**  
There is no fixed time limit, but **earlier is better**. Once your order is packed and handed over to the courier,
it may no longer be possible to cancel it. At that point, you may need to treat it as a **return** after delivery.

**Q16. Will I be charged for cancelling my order before it ships?**  
In most cases, there are **no cancellation fees** if the order is cancelled before shipping.
Any authorized payments are **voided or refunded** to your original payment method.

---

### 5. Cancellations – After Shipping or Delivery

**Q17. Can I cancel an order that has already shipped?**  
Once an order has shipped, full cancellation is often **not possible**. However, you can:
- Decline the delivery (if your local courier allows it), or
- Accept the package and then initiate a **return** according to the returns policy.

**Q18. Can I cancel part of my order (only some items)?**  
Partial cancellations depend on the **fulfilment stage** and **warehouse constraints**.
If the items have not yet been packed or shipped, you may cancel individual items. If not, you might need to return
unwanted items after delivery.

**Q19. What if my order is delayed—can I cancel it then?**  
In many situations, you can request a **cancellation or compensation** if your order is significantly delayed.
The exact handling may depend on carrier investigations and internal rules, but customer support will try to provide
a **fair resolution** (refund, voucher, or replacement).

---

### 6. Interaction with the Chatbot

**Q20. What can the chatbot do regarding returns and cancellations?**  
The chatbot can:
- Explain the **returns, refunds, and cancellations policies** based on this FAQ and the policy documents.
- Help you **start a return request** for eligible items.
- Help you **raise a cancellation request** before shipping (subject to system and policy rules).
- Provide **status updates** by querying your orders and returns through **SQL tools**.

The chatbot **cannot**:
- Guarantee approval of returns or cancellations.
- Override decisions made by human agents.
- Modify bank timelines for refunds.

**Q21. Will the chatbot send me emails about my returns or cancellations?**  
Yes. When you request a **return** or **cancellation** through the chatbot, the system:
- Creates or updates the relevant database entries (e.g., `returns`, `tickets`).
- Sends a **confirmation email** to your registered email address.
- Logs the email in an `email_logs` table for auditing and tracking.

**Q22. Is a human involved in my return or cancellation request?**  
Yes. This system is designed with **human-in-the-loop**:
- A human **admin** reviews return requests and certain complex cancellation cases.
- The admin may approve, reject, or edit requests and leave internal notes.
- The chatbot explains that a **ticket has been raised** and that a **human agent** will review your case.

- Retrieve the **most relevant answers** from this FAQ and related policy documents.
- Present concise, user-friendly information that is **grounded** in this text.
- Clearly separate **general policy information** from **order-specific status updates**, which must use SQL tools.
