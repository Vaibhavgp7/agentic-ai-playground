## Shipping Policy

### 1. Overview

This document describes the **shipping and delivery policy** for the fictional e‑commerce store used in your capstone project.
It exists to support RAG-based answers about delivery timelines, shipping fees, and related topics.

### 2. Shipping Regions

- The store currently ships to:
  - All major metropolitan and Tier-1 cities in the supported country.
  - Selected Tier-2 and Tier-3 cities, depending on courier coverage.
- Remote or hard-to-reach locations may have:
  - **Longer delivery timelines**.
  - **Limited carrier options**.
  - **Additional surcharges** in some cases.

### 3. Shipping Methods and Delivery Times

- **Standard Shipping**
  - Typical delivery time: **3–7 business days** from the date of dispatch.
  - Available for most products and locations.
  - Tracking information is provided once the order is shipped.

- **Express Shipping**
  - Typical delivery time: **1–3 business days** from the date of dispatch.
  - Available only for eligible products and pin codes.
  - Additional fees apply; fees are shown at checkout.

- **Same-Day or Next-Day Delivery**
  - Available only in select cities and for selected items.
  - Cut-off times may apply (for example, orders placed before 12:00 PM).
  - Delivery typically occurs **within 24 hours** of order confirmation.

These timelines are **estimates** and may be affected by factors such as:
- Public holidays.
- Weather disruptions.
- Courier capacity and operational constraints.

### 4. Order Processing and Dispatch

- **Order confirmation**
  - Once an order is placed and payment is authorized, the customer receives an **order confirmation email**.

- **Processing time**
  - Most orders are processed and handed over to the courier within **1–2 business days**.
  - Orders with large or bulky items, or orders requiring additional verification, may take longer.

- **Split shipments**
  - An order containing multiple items may be shipped in **separate packages** depending on:
    - Inventory location.
    - Supplier or warehouse.
  - Customers receive tracking information for each shipment.

### 5. Shipping Fees

- **Standard Shipping**
  - May be **free** above a certain order value (e.g., free shipping for orders over a specific amount).
  - For smaller orders, a flat or region-based fee may apply.

- **Express Shipping**
  - Always charged as an additional fee, calculated based on:
    - Weight and volume.
    - Destination pin code.
    - Carrier charges.

- **Special surcharges**
  - Remote area fees.
  - Oversized item handling fees.

At checkout, all **applicable shipping charges** are displayed before the customer confirms the order.

### 6. Tracking and Delivery Updates

- **Tracking information**
  - Once the order ships, a tracking ID and a tracking link are provided via:
    - Email.
    - The “My Orders” section of the customer account.

- **Delivery notifications**
  - Customers may receive:
    - “Out for delivery” notifications on the scheduled delivery day.
    - Delivery confirmation after the package has been delivered.

- **Failed delivery attempts**
  - If the courier cannot deliver the package (e.g., due to incorrect address or unavailability), they may:
    - Attempt **redelivery**.
    - Contact the customer for updated information.
    - Return the package to the sender after multiple failed attempts.

### 7. Delayed or Lost Shipments

- **Shipping delays**
  - If a shipment is delayed beyond the typical delivery window, customers should:
    - Check tracking information.
    - Contact support via chatbot or help center.
  - The support team may:
    - Investigate with the courier.
    - Provide updated delivery estimates.
    - Offer partial refunds or vouchers in some cases (depending on internal policy).

- **Lost or missing packages**
  - If a tracking status shows “Delivered” but the customer did not receive the package:
    - Customers should first check with neighbors, security desks, or family members.
    - If still not found, the support team opens an **investigation ticket** with the courier.
  - After investigation:
    - A **replacement order** may be shipped, or
    - A **refund** may be issued, depending on the findings and policy.

### 8. Address Changes and Cancellations Before Dispatch

- **Changing the shipping address**
  - Address changes are best requested **before dispatch**.
  - Once an order is shipped, address changes might not be possible or may require contacting the courier directly.

- **Cancelling an order before shipping**
  - If the order has not yet been shipped, customers can typically **cancel the order** with full refund.
  - The chatbot may assist in **raising a cancellation request**, which is then processed by the system.

### 9. How the Chatbot Should Use This Policy

- For general shipping questions like:
  - “How long does shipping take?”
  - “Do you offer express delivery?”
  - “Why is my order delayed?”
  - “How much is the shipping fee?”
  - “When will I get tracking information?”
- The chatbot should use **RAG with this shipping policy document** to provide grounded, policy-based answers.

- For **order-specific** questions like:
  - “What is the status of my order #123?”
  - “Is my order shipped yet?”
  - “What is the tracking number for my order?”
- The chatbot must rely on **SQL tools** querying the `orders`, `order_items`, and related tables, rather than on this generic policy text.
